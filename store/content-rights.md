# ForkIt launch content rights

Rights and source pages checked November 18, 2026 for version 1.0.

| Content | Source and conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Nearby restaurant data and photos | Google Places supplies restaurant details and venue photos for active rooms. The swipe and match screens credit Google Maps, link any third-party provider, and show the individual photo and contributor links supplied by Google. Directions uses Google Maps. Match notifications retain the place ID and retrieve details from the live room. [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) and [Maps service terms](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms) |
| Suggested dishes | ForkIt’s cuisine-based suggestions are marked as suggestions. The 113 example JPEGs have per-file source, creator, license, and byte-version records in [the manifest](../server/dish-photos.json). Each stored file matched its recorded hash, and each recorded license matched its Commons file page. Two files carry custom creator credits. Commons photos remain available under their individual licenses after resizing. [Commons reuse guide](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) |
| Ceviche Tostada sample card | John Comeau photographed the tostadas in Puerto Vallarta. ForkIt crops and resizes the photograph and distributes the derivative under CC BY-SA 3.0. The card carries its credit, license, and source. [Original photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tostadas_de_ceviche.jpg) and [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |
| Cook In recipes and menus | The 20 recipe methods and quantities were written for ForkIt. Sample restaurants are fictional. The US chain list identifies signature dishes using menu-item names. ForkIt does not claim an affiliation with those chains. Spoonacular lookup is disabled by default. Optional Gemini Maps-grounded results require returned source links. |
| Brand and UI | The developer confirmed commercial rights to the supplied app icon and splash artwork. Poppins uses SIL OFL 1.1, Ionicons and Feather use MIT, and the Material Design icon font uses Apache 2.0. Copies of the relevant licenses are in [artwork notices](../assets/artwork-notices.json) and the app. |
| App Store screenshots | The two food photographs visible in the iPhone screenshots are Chicken Parmesan and Spicy Pad Thai. The records below match the actual CDN asset IDs to their original photo pages. The group lobby, setup, and recipe screens use ForkIt’s interface and copy. |
