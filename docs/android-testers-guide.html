Part 1

Install the app

New testers start with the signup form. If your ForkIt install email has arrived, go straight to step 2 on your Android phone.

ⓘUse the same Google account for every step: the one your phone's Play Store uses. If you have more than one, check which one the Play Store shows when you tap your profile picture.

  1. Request your test link

    Open the form with the Google account you use in the Play Store. Confirm the two testing questions and submit.

    Sign up for the Android testOpens Google Forms ›

    Sean will add your account and email you after Google Play has had time to update. Look for Your ForkIt Android test link. Continue when it arrives.

  2. Become a tester

    After your install email arrives, open its link and tap Become a tester. You can also use this button.

    Opt in to the testOpens Google Play in your browser ›

    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.seandm.forkit

    Once Google confirms you’re a tester, follow its link to Google Play or use step 3.

    ↩ Then come back here for step 3

  3. Install from Google Play

    Tap the green button. It opens ForkIt in the Play Store. Tap Install. ForkIt appears with your other apps, with the crossed-forks icon.

    Get ForkIt on Google PlayOpens the Play Store ›

    Says "Item not found"?

    Check your Google account.

    Use the same account in your browser and the Play Store. You can refresh the store on your phone:

    1. Open Settings, tap Apps, then Google Play Store, then Force stop.
    2. Come back here and tap the green button again.

    Still not found? Check that the Play Store is signed in to the same Google account you used in steps 1 and 2 (tap your profile picture in the Play Store). If it is, wait half an hour and try again.

  4. Keep it for 14 days

    This part matters most

    Please keep ForkIt installed and stay opted in for at least 14 days.

    Google counts testers who stay opted in continuously for the full 14 days after they join.

    So for two weeks: keep the app on your phone and avoid tapping Leave the program. Opening it every few days helps too.

Part 2

Using the App

Best with 2 or more people. You can also start a room by yourself just to look around. iPhone friends can swipe in the same room.

  1. Type your first name

    Then tap Let's Eat. A short How ForkIt works walkthrough opens the first time. Go through it or tap Skip; the ? button on the next screen brings it back.

  2. Choose Dine Out

    Tap Enable Location, then While using the app. ForkIt only uses it to find restaurants near you. If Google also asks to turn on location accuracy, either answer works. Then choose:

    Deciding: breakfast, lunch or dinner. Only places open for that meal show up.

    Cards: By place gives one card per restaurant, so fewer swipes. By dish gives every dish its own card.

    Search Radius: how far it looks.

  3. One person makes a room

    The host taps Create Room and gets a 6-character code. Tap the share button at the top to send the invite.

    Everyone else taps the link they were sent, or taps Join Room and types the code.

    Everyone in the room needs ForkIt installed from the test. If someone doesn't have it yet, send them the signup form first.

  4. Allow notifications

    In the waiting room, tap Allow when asked so you hear when the group finds a match. When everyone is in, the host taps Start Swiping.

  5. Swipe right on dinner

    Every card shows a dish at a real restaurant nearby. Swipe right if you'd eat it, left if not, or use the heart and X buttons. The button between them undoes your last swipe.

    On a By place card, tap the right or left side of the photo to see that restaurant's other dishes, then swipe on the one showing.

    NOPE← left · right →YUMMY

  6. It's a match

    When most of the group swipes right on the same restaurant, everyone sees IT'S A MATCH. From there you can get directions, order delivery through DoorDash or Uber Eats, or open Google Maps, plus call or visit the website when the restaurant lists them.

  7. No match? Go again

    If everyone swipes every card and nothing matches, anyone can tap Find New Places to get restaurants farther out, or Shuffle and Go Again when there are no new ones.

In the middle of swiping? Tap the circles with everyone's initials at the top. You'll see the room code to invite more people, how far everyone has got, and your own swipes. Nobody else sees how you voted.

Cooking at home instead? Pick Cook In in step 2 to swipe on recipes. No location needed. The match comes with the full recipe: change the servings and the amounts follow, tick off ingredients, and share a shopping list.

Part 3

Give Sean Feedback

Use ForkIt for real when you're deciding where to eat, then tell Sean anything that stood out. Short is fine: "the photo for the burger was a salad" helps a lot.

What to look for

  • Are the dishes real?

    Do they sound like things that restaurant actually serves? Anything odd, like drinks, breakfast at dinner time, or a dish they don't have?

  • Do the photos fit?

    Does the picture look like the dish? Did you see the same photo over and over?

  • Are the restaurants right?

    Close by and places you'd actually go? Any favorites missing, or places that have closed?

  • Was joining easy?

    Did the code or link work for everyone? Did anyone get stuck in the waiting room?

  • Did the match work?

    Did everyone get the match and the notification? Did directions, delivery, call and website work?

  • Anything confusing?

    A screen where you weren't sure what to tap, or text that was hard to read.

  • Anything broken?

    The app closing by itself, freezing, or loading forever. Say what you were doing when it happened.

  • Would you use it?

    Would your group use this to pick dinner? What would make you want to?

How to send it

  1. Easiest: text Sean

    A quick message or a screenshot is perfect. To take a screenshot, press the power and volume-down buttons together.

  2. Or leave a note on Google Play

    Open ForkIt's page in the Play Store and write a review. While you're a tester, it goes privately to Sean instead of being posted.

  3. If it crashes

    If your phone says ForkIt keeps stopping, close it and text Sean what you were doing. Google usually sends Sean the crash details on its own.

If something goes wrong

"Become a tester" says the app isn't available

Wait for your install email and check that your browser and Play Store both use the account you submitted. Retry the email link. If it still fails, reply to the email so Sean can check your access.

The Play Store says "Item not found"

Force stop the Play Store (Settings, Apps, Google Play Store, Force stop) and tap the install button again. If that doesn't work, tap your profile picture in the Play Store and check it’s the account you used on the signup form. If it is, reopen the opt-in link from the install email or reply for help.

The cards say "Sample restaurant"

Those are made-up places, shown when ForkIt can't use real ones. Location may be off for ForkIt. Open Settings, tap Apps, then ForkIt, Permissions, Location, and choose Allow only while using the app. Then make a new room. If location is already on, nothing may be open for that meal nearby, so try a new room with a bigger Search Radius.

I left the app in the middle of a room

You stay in the room for 15 minutes and your swipes still count, so just go back to ForkIt. If the app closed, tap the invite link again, or tap Join Room and type the code.

I can't find the app on my phone

Swipe up from the bottom of your home screen to see all your apps, and search "ForkIt". You can also open it from its page in the Play Store.

The room code doesn't work

Rooms close when everyone leaves, after an hour with no activity, and sometimes when Sean updates the app. Ask the host to make a new room.

How do I get updates?

Most fixes arrive by themselves: ForkIt downloads them when you open it and uses them the next time you open it. Bigger updates come through the Play Store like any other app.
