Part 1

Install the app

Do these on your Android phone, in this order. Each button opens a new page, so come back here after each one.

ⓘUse the same Google account for every step: the one your phone's Play Store uses. If you have more than one, check which one the Play Store shows when you tap your profile picture.

  1. Join the ForkIt testers group

    This is how Google knows you're allowed to test. Tap the button, sign in if asked, then tap Join group.

    Join the testers groupOpens Google Groups ›

    groups.google.com/g/forkit-testers

    You won't get emails from it. It's only a guest list.

    ↩ Then come back here for step 2

  2. Become a tester

    Tap the button, then tap Become a tester on the page that opens.

    Opt in to the testOpens Google Play in your browser ›

    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.seandm.forkit

    If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

    ↩ Then come back here for step 3

  3. Install from Google Play

    Tap the green button. It opens ForkIt in the Play Store. Tap Install. ForkIt appears with your other apps, with the crossed-forks icon.

    Get ForkIt on Google PlayOpens the Play Store ›

    If the Play Store says the item isn't found, step 2 hasn't caught up yet. Wait a few minutes and tap the button again.

  4. Keep it for 14 days

    This part matters most

    Please keep ForkIt installed for at least 14 days.

    Google counts how many testers stay in the test for 14 days in a row. If people drop out, the count starts over and ForkIt can't go public yet.

    So for two weeks: don't uninstall it, don't leave the group, and don't tap Leave the program. Opening it every few days helps too.

Part 2

Using the App

Best with 2 or more people. You can also start a room by yourself just to look around. iPhone friends can swipe in the same room.

  1. Type your first name

    Then tap Let's Eat.

  2. Choose Dine Out

    When your phone asks about location, tap While using the app. ForkIt only uses it to find restaurants near you. If Google also asks to turn on location accuracy, either answer works. Search Radius sets how far it looks.

  3. One person makes a room

    The host taps Create Room and gets a 6-letter code. Tap the code to send it to the group.

    Everyone else taps the link they were sent, or taps Join Room and types the code.

    Everyone in the room needs ForkIt installed from the test. If someone doesn't have it yet, send them this page first.

  4. Allow notifications

    In the waiting room, tap Allow when asked so you hear when the group finds a match. When everyone is in, the host taps Start Swiping.

  5. Swipe right on dinner

    Each card is one dish at a real restaurant nearby. Swipe right if you'd eat it, left if not.

    NOPE← left · right →YUMMY

  6. It's a match

    When most of the group swipes right on the same restaurant, everyone sees IT'S A MATCH. From there you can get directions, call, or open the restaurant's website.

Cooking at home instead? Pick Cook In in step 2 to swipe on recipes. No location needed.

Part 3

Give Sean Feedback

Use ForkIt for real when you're deciding where to eat, then tell Sean anything that stood out. Short is fine: "the photo for the burger was a salad" helps a lot.

What to look for

  • Are the dishes real?

    Do they sound like things that restaurant actually serves? Anything odd, like drinks, breakfast at dinner time, or a dish they don't have?

  • Do the photos fit?

    Does the picture look like the dish? Did you see the same photo over and over?

  • Are the restaurants right?

    Close by and places you'd actually go? Any favorites missing, or places that have closed?

  • Was joining easy?

    Did the code or link work for everyone? Did anyone get stuck in the waiting room?

  • Did the match work?

    Did everyone get the match and the notification? Did directions, call and website work?

  • Anything confusing?

    A screen where you weren't sure what to tap, or text that was hard to read.

  • Anything broken?

    The app closing by itself, freezing, or loading forever. Say what you were doing when it happened.

  • Would you use it?

    Would your group use this to pick dinner? What would make you want to?

How to send it

  1. Easiest: text Sean

    A quick message or a screenshot is perfect. To take a screenshot, press the power and volume-down buttons together.

  2. Or leave a note on Google Play

    Open ForkIt's page in the Play Store and write a review. While you're a tester, it goes privately to Sean instead of being posted.

  3. If it crashes

    If your phone says ForkIt keeps stopping, close it and text Sean what you were doing. Google usually sends Sean the crash details on its own.

If something goes wrong

"Become a tester" says the app isn't available

Make sure you joined the testers group in step 1 with the same Google account you're signed in with now. Then wait a few minutes and open the step 2 link again.

The Play Store says "Item not found"

Tap your profile picture in the Play Store and check it's the account you joined the group with. If it is, wait a few minutes after step 2 and try again.

No restaurants show up

Location may be off for ForkIt. Open Settings, tap Apps, then ForkIt, Permissions, Location, and choose Allow only while using the app. Or try a bigger Search Radius.

I can't find the app on my phone

Swipe up from the bottom of your home screen to see all your apps, and search "ForkIt". You can also open it from its page in the Play Store.

The room code doesn't work

Rooms close when everyone leaves, and sometimes when Sean updates the app. Ask the host to make a new room.

How do I get updates?

Most fixes arrive by themselves: ForkIt downloads them when you open it and uses them the next time you open it. Bigger updates come through the Play Store like any other app.
