APP_NAME_PLACEHOLDER has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is written to a database.

What the app collects

  • A display name. Whatever you type when you open the app. It is stored on your own device and sent to our server so the other people in your session can see who joined.
  • Your location, only in "dine out" mode. If you grant location permission, your coordinates and chosen search radius are sent to our server once, used immediately to look up nearby restaurants, and then discarded. They are never saved.
  • Your swipes. Which dishes you liked or passed on during a session, so the app can work out what the group agrees on.
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications. A random identifier for your device's notifications, sent to our server when a game starts so it can tell you when your group finds a match, even if you have left the app.

What the app does not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password — there are no accounts.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, or microphone data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No background or continuous location. Location is read once, on request.

How long it is kept

Session data — display names, swipes, notification tokens, and the generated dish list — is held in the server's memory only, for the length of the session. It is deleted when the last person leaves, when the server restarts, or automatically after one hour, whichever comes first. There is no database and no backup, so once a session ends the data is gone.

Your display name and preferred search radius are also stored locally on your device so you do not have to retype them. Deleting the app removes them.

Who else sees it

  • Other people in your session see your display name and, while swiping, that you voted — not what you voted for, until a match is found.
  • Google Places API receives your coordinates and search radius in "dine out" mode, in order to return nearby restaurants. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API receives your coordinates and the names and addresses of nearby restaurants in "dine out" mode, to suggest popular dishes at each one using Google Maps. Your name is not sent. This is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • spoonacular receives the names of nearby restaurants in "dine out" mode, to look up real menu items at chain restaurants. Your name and location are not sent.
  • Expo, Apple and Google deliver match notifications if you allow them. Our server sends your notification token and the matched restaurant or recipe to Expo's push service, which passes it to Apple Push Notification service on iPhone or Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android to show on your device. This is governed by Expo's Privacy Policy and Apple's or Google's.
  • Railway hosts the server and processes traffic on our behalf.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Children

APP_NAME_PLACEHOLDER is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Notification permission can be declined or turned off at any time in your device settings; the app works the same without it. Location permission can be declined or revoked at any time in your device settings; "cook in" mode works without it, and "dine out" falls back to a built-in list of dishes. To remove the data stored on your device, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Because sessions are never persisted, there is no stored history for us to delete on request.
