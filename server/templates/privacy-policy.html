APP_NAME_PLACEHOLDER has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is written to a database.

What the app collects

  • A display name. Whatever you type when you open the app. It is stored on your own device and sent to our server so the other people in your session can see who joined.
  • Your approximate location, only in "dine out" mode. If you grant location permission, your coordinates and chosen search radius are sent to our server once, used immediately to look up nearby restaurants, and then discarded. They are never saved.
  • Your swipes. Which dishes you liked or passed on during a session, so the app can work out what the group agrees on.

What the app does not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password — there are no accounts.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, or microphone data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No background or continuous location. Location is read once, on request.

How long it is kept

Session data — display names, swipes, and the generated dish list — is held in the server's memory only, for the length of the session. It is deleted when the last person leaves, when the server restarts, or automatically after one hour, whichever comes first. There is no database and no backup, so once a session ends the data is gone.

Your display name and preferred search radius are also stored locally on your device so you do not have to retype them. Deleting the app removes them.

Who else sees it

  • Other people in your session see your display name and, while swiping, that you voted — not what you voted for, until a match is found.
  • Google Places API receives your coordinates and search radius in "dine out" mode, in order to return nearby restaurants. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Railway hosts the server and processes traffic on our behalf.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Children

APP_NAME_PLACEHOLDER is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Location permission can be declined or revoked at any time in your device settings; "cook in" mode works without it, and "dine out" falls back to a built-in list of dishes. To remove the data stored on your device, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Because sessions are never persisted, there is no stored history for us to delete on request.
