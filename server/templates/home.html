How it works

  1. 1

    Start a room

    Create a room and share the 6-character code or invite link with your group.

  2. 2

    Swipe on dishes

    Everyone swipes right on the dishes they'd eat and left on the ones they wouldn't.

  3. 3

    Get a match

    When a majority likes the same place, the app calls it and shows you the way.

Dine out finds restaurants near you and the dishes they're known for. Cook in picks a recipe to make at home. No account needed, just a name.
